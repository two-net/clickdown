    ClickDown

    Keyboard shortcuts

    ↑↓ or JKMove the selection
    Enter or →Open the selected item
    Esc, Backspace or ←Go up one level
    Home EndFirst or last item
    Page Up Page DownMove ten items, or scroll a task
    RRefresh this screen
    OOpen the task in ClickUp
    LShow or hide the logs
    ?Show or hide this list

    ClickDown is read-only, so none of these can change anything in ClickUp.
